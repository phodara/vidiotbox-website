COSMIC SINGULARITY HAIKUS

Event Horizon
Light bends toward the dark
The last star slips out of sight
No farewell comes back

At the Center
All roads lead inward
Space folds its maps into dust
Time forgets the way

Collapse
A great star gives way
Its heart falls beyond all reach
Night keeps what remains

Infinite
Numbers climb and break
Our equations lose their hold
Darkness gives no hint

Before Dawn
We trace light backward
Till our oldest stories fail
At the edge of time

Silent Orbit
Stars circle a hush
Their bright paths reveal a weight
No eye looks upon

Boundary
One last photon speeds
Along the rim of return
Then the dark takes all

Unknown
Where all measures end
We leave a blank on the page
And call it a point

Unmeasured
My ruler lies still
Beside a sky with no seam
Time rests on its marks

Scale
A grain on my palm
Outweighs the worlds in my mind
I let the grain fall

Not a Place
I ask where it is
But what is folded inward?
Rain blurs lines of thought

Long Fall
A stone leaves my hand
I count until counting fails
Infinity's end

Small Mind
The mind makes a room
For what has no walls at all
Then calls it too small

Silk
Space strains like thin silk
Through an eye no thread can find
I lose sight of all

Watch
I wind the brass watch
And ask it to keep the time
Where no clock can count

Lens
Through the curved glass lens
I seek the end of all scale
My breath blurs the glass

Unwritten
The proof breaks in two
Between the ink and the page
My pen holds nothing

Vastness
Past all miles I know
A depth no distance can name
Opens in one breath

Compressed
Ten thousand night skies
Fold in the crease of a thought
Still I cannot sleep

Ratio
I weigh dust and suns
The balance tilts past reason
Dawn lifts both my hands

No Outside
I draw one more ring
To fence the end of all things
The blank still goes on

Unlearning
Each name falls away
From the dark I thought I knew
Rain speaks through the leaves

Rod of Light
Past the reach of thought
Night breaks the last rod of light
Hands open like stars

Salt
Mountains turn to mist
Mist shrinks to a grain of salt
Salt slips through the mind

Rimless
Circles lose their rims
Vastness has no place to stand
Still, this soul comes home

Needle
A pin pierces night
Through its eye, the stars pour in
Nothingness comes loose

Black Thread
A thread draws worlds close
Till no gap is left for thought
The knot is my heart

Last Rung
Stars form a staircase
Each rung leads past height and depth
My feet slip away

Kneeling
No shrine marks this place
Yet my feet have found the ground
Beneath the bright stars

Prayer
I bring no request
To the point that swallows light
Just this breath is all

Candle
One flame in the room
The dark beyond thought stays dark
I trim the charred wick

Surrender
I unclench my fist
The question keeps its own shape
I need not hold it

Communion
My breath clouds the glass
Through which I watch soft starlight
For once, near is far

Pilgrim
No shrine waits for me
Only the pull of a point
And stones in my shoes

Hollow Bell
I strike the bronze bell
Its note thins against the dark
My ribs hold the hum

Black Altar
A black bead of space
Holds the weight of unnamed suns
I kneel without words

Wordless
Mouth poised for a chant
At the point where words grow dim
The hush sings through me

Clear Bell
The bell makes no sound
Yet its hush fills all of space
My soul hears the note

Grace
The dark yields no proof
Still I rise to make the tea
And set out two cups

No Size
I picture a point
Then erase its width and depth
What is left to see?

Without End
I count without end
Each number still has one more
There is no last step

Humility
Before such vastness
I need not stand at the heart
Of all that exists

Reverence
A dead star's dark heart
Exceeds the reach of my mind
I bow to the dark
