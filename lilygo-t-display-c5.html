PocketProwler

LilyGO T-Display C5 WiFi/BLE Analyzer v2.1.0

PocketProwler is a WiFi and Bluetooth scanner for the LilyGO T-Display C5, with firmware developed in PlatformIO. It scans nearby WiFi networks, shows SSID, RSSI, signal bars, channel, band, and security, and adds a Bluetooth scanner mode with passive and active BLE options. The handheld build fits into a custom 3D-printed enclosure designed for the T-Display C5 with a 3000 mAh LiPo.

WiFi

Scan nearby networks and inspect radio conditions from the handheld display.

BLE

Shows nearby BLE names, address details, RSSI, signal bars, last-seen age, and RSSI history.

Buttons

Use the upper button to scroll, the lower button to cycle views, and both buttons together to switch WiFi/Bluetooth modes.
