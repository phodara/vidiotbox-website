What is running?

The first visit downloads a roughly 15 MB model. Generation then runs entirely in a background worker on your device. No prompt or poem is sent to a server.

This uses the device’s V8.3 release (V8 model), INT4 weights, INT8 activation runtime, and shared grammar, syllable, theme, voice, and repetition rules. It is a WebAssembly build of the C++ host decoder, with no cloud model or AI repair step.

Speed and random choices differ from the physical device. Recent-word history is kept per theme until you reload; a case number does not reset it. Space poems also skip any line already used in the last 32 Space poems, as on the V8 device, unless no other line fits. Auto mode waits 40 seconds between generations. Form checks do not guarantee poetic coherence.
