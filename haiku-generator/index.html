What is running?

The first visit downloads a roughly 15 MB model. Generation then runs entirely in a background worker on your device. No prompt or poem is sent to a server.

This uses the device’s V8.17 release (V8 model), INT4 weights, INT8 activation runtime, and shared grammar, syllable, theme, voice, and repetition rules. It is a WebAssembly build of the C++ host decoder, with no cloud model or AI repair step.

Speed and random choices differ from the physical device. Recent-word history is kept per theme until you reload; a case number does not reset it. Space poems also skip any line already used in the last 32 Space poems, as on the V8 device, unless no other line fits. Auto mode waits 40 seconds between generations. Form checks do not guarantee poetic coherence.

Free is the device’s own generator. One-thing and Two-part are the haiku lab’s styles, written by the same model and V8.17 rules with a style step added: One-thing keeps every line to one concept’s space words; Two-part writes line one from them and lines 2–3 from the concept’s earthly image. Their space lines leave out technical words (fusion, atoms, density, radio waves), Two-part’s first line always names something in space, and both prefer a line with an action to one built on “is”. They write Space poems only and have their own recent-word history.
