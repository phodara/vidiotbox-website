ABOUT THE LAB

Every poem here is written on your device by the same tiny model and grammar as the Vidiotbox C5 board. The lab compares ways of making the three lines belong together.

V8.16 is the main page's generator as of 27 October, kept unchanged here for comparison. One-thing keeps every line to one concept, such as ancient light, scale or silence. Two-part sets a cosmic first line against an earthly image after the cut, the way a traditional haiku sets two images side by side. Blind mix picks one of the three at random and tells you which after you rate it.

Your ratings go to the project with the name you entered, and a copy stays in this browser.
